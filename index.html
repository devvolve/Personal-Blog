# about

I'm Daniyal — a software engineer who spends most working hours on backend systems and LLM-driven workflows, and most free hours either reading model papers I probably should've read in school, or breaking a game prototype in some new and educational way.

I studied computer science end to end: undergrad at UC San Diego, a minor in CS alongside it, and a master's at Cal Poly Pomona. What's kept me interested since is less the theory and more the feedback loop — training something, shipping something, watching it fail in a way that teaches you something specific.

This site is where I write that down before I forget it.
